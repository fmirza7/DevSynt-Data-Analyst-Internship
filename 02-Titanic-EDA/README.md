# Titanic EDA

Exploratory data analysis of the Titanic dataset using Python, focusing on passenger survival patterns and relationships between demographic and travel characteristics.

## 📊 Overview

This project includes:

* Data exploration and feature engineering
* Survival analysis by gender, class, age, fare, family size, travelling status, and embarkation port
* Descriptive statistics and correlation analysis
* Data visualizations and a summary dashboard
* Business insights and recommendations

## 🛠️ Tools

**Python · Pandas · NumPy · Matplotlib · Seaborn · Jupyter Notebook**

## 💡 Key Findings

* **38.38%** of passengers survived.
* Female passengers had a much higher survival rate than males (**74.20% vs. 18.89%**).
* First-class passengers had a higher survival rate than third-class passengers (**62.96% vs. 24.24%**).
* High-fare passengers had a higher survival rate than low-fare passengers (**76.29% vs. 28.73%**).
* Passengers travelling with family had a higher survival rate than those travelling alone (**50.56% vs. 30.35%**).
